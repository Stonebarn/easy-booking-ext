Privacy Policy — Dialer Helper Pro

Effective 6 August 2026 · Wiza Inc. · Contact: jack.foley@wiza.com

Dialer Helper Pro is an internal tool for Wiza's own sales team. It is a Chrome side-panel extension that shows a sales representative their prospect's CRM context during dialer calls, fills a meeting-booking form, and syncs the representative's call notes to Wiza's HubSpot CRM. It is not offered to, or useful for, anyone outside Wiza: every feature requires a Wiza HubSpot account.

What the extension processes

  • Prospect details from the dialer page the representative is viewing (name, company, email address, phone number, timezone, job title, LinkedIn profile link and photo, and CRM record IDs). These are read from the page, held locally in the representative's browser, and used to look up and display the matching records from Wiza's HubSpot CRM.
  • Call notes the representative saves in the dialer, which the extension writes to the matching contact and company records in Wiza's HubSpot CRM, attributed to the signed-in representative.
  • The representative's HubSpot sign-in: OAuth tokens for their own HubSpot account, stored only in the browser's local extension storage on their device.
  • Extension settings (for example, whether notes auto-sync), stored locally.

Where data goes

  • HubSpot (api.hubapi.com): CRM records are read from, and call notes are written to, Wiza's own HubSpot account — the same CRM the data already lives in. HubSpot's handling of that data is governed by HubSpot's privacy policy and Wiza's agreement with HubSpot.
  • Wiza's sign-in service (wiza-hs-connect.lovable.app): used solely to complete the HubSpot OAuth token exchange, so no authentication secret ever ships inside the extension. Tokens transit this service only for the exchange itself.
  • Google's favicon service (google.com/s2/favicons): the extension requests a company's public favicon by domain name to display a logo. The company domain is the only data sent.
  • LinkedIn's image CDN: when the dialer page shows a prospect's LinkedIn photo, the browser fetches that image to display it in the panel.

That is the complete list. The extension contains no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no data collection by Wiza beyond what is described above.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or transfer any data to third parties.
  • We do not use data for any purpose unrelated to the extension's single purpose (showing CRM context and syncing call notes).
  • We do not use data to determine creditworthiness or for lending.
  • We do not collect browsing history, keystrokes outside the dialer's notes, or anything from pages other than the dialer, the booking form, and HubSpot's API.

Storage and retention

Everything the extension stores lives in Chrome's local extension storage on the representative's own device. Disconnecting HubSpot in the extension's settings deletes the stored tokens; uninstalling the extension deletes all locally stored data. Notes written to HubSpot become CRM records governed by Wiza's internal data-retention practices.

Changes and contact

If the extension's data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date revised. Questions: jack.foley@wiza.com.

Dialer Helper Pro is developed and used exclusively by Wiza Inc. for its internal sales operations.
